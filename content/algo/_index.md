## Three ways to learn {#how}

**Green: interview core, written right and fast.** Interviews and easy contest problems test writing familiar things correctly in one go, so this block is learned by solving a few problems of each kind until the templates need no thought.

**Grey: skim.** Segment trees, flows, suffix automata and the like are rarely asked in interviews and are not my focus in contests. Know what each solves, what it costs and which signal points to it, and be able to use a template when needed.

**Blue and yellow: the contest focus, mathematics.** Number theory, counting and linear algebra are learned to the point of deriving formulas and stating the idea on the spot. Blue is the main line, top to bottom; yellow boxes are picked up when the main line reaches their arrow. Every block comes with problems, to practise recognising which tool a statement calls for.
